# BookVerse datasets

This folder is intended to contain the three CSV datasets used in the BookVerse Retail Business Intelligence & Sales Analytics project.

| File | Records | Columns |
|---|---:|---:|
| Books.csv | 500 | 7 |
| Customers.csv | 500 | 6 |
| Orders.csv | 500 | 6 |

## Privacy

Before publication, the customer Name, Email, and Phone fields were replaced with synthetic placeholders. Customer_ID values were preserved so joins to Orders remain valid. Books and Orders were retained as supplied.

These are project datasets for analysis and demonstration, not official business records.
